# Pack Rat — Domain Glossary

## Game Concept
A visual incremental/idle game about a rat building a nest in an abandoned attic. The player clicks on scattered junk to send the rat scavenging, then uses collected resources to build nest zones and unlock automation. Progress through 5 attic tiers, each larger and more complex, culminating in **Ratopia**.

## Core Interactions

- **Scavenge** — click on a junk item in the attic to send the rat to grab it. The rat visibly scurries to the item and drags it back to the nest.
- **Assign rats** — drag rats to nest zones for specialized resource production. Unassigned rats forage automatically.
- **Build zones** — construct nest facilities (Food Storage, Nursery, Bait Workshop, etc.) to unlock new capabilities.
- **Respond to threats** — disable traps, avoid poison, distract Kuzco.

## Attic Progression

5 attics, player-initiated move via "Explore the vent" button when goals are met. On move: nest/resources reset, rats and knowledge carry over.

| Attic | Rat Cap | Goal | Est. Time |
|-------|---------|------|-----------|
| **The Eaves** | 1 (you) | Mating Display → attract Mate | ~1-2h |
| **The Storage Room** | 2-3 (you + mate) | Survive traps + poison, produce Litter | ~3-5h |
| **The Ballroom** | 5-6 | Unlock crafting, occasional Kuzco visits | ~4-6h |
| **The Grand Attic** | 10-12 | Build Cat Barrier, Kuzco as major hurdle | ~5-7h |
| **Ratopia** | Unlimited | Ultimate nest, ending | ~2-5h |

**Total playtime:** ~15-25h main, ~40h completionist.
**Cost scaling:** ~2.5x resource requirements per attic tier.

## Automation

- **Rat #1 (you)** — always manual. The player character.
- **Mate** (Rat #2, unlocked via Mating Display in Storage Room) — first auto-scavenge, but slow.
- **Babies** (Litter upgrade) — auto-scavenge faster than mate.
- **Colony** — most rats auto, player sets zone priorities.

Automation never fully replaces clicking. New junk spawns regularly to encourage active play.

## Nest Zones

Constructable facilities placed visually in the attic. Each zone unlocks or boosts a function:

| Zone | Unlocked | Function |
|------|----------|----------|
| **Food Storage** | Eaves | Increases Food cap, boosts Food production |
| **Materials Pile** | Eaves | Increases Fluff/Scrap/Shiny cap |
| **Library** | Eaves | Research retention on attic move. Level determines % carried over |
| **Mating Display** | Eaves | Win structure — attracts mate, gates Storage Room |
| **Nursery** | Storage Room | Required for Litter (baby rats) |
| **Infirmary** | Ballroom | Poison recovery, safe poison collection |
| **Bait Workshop** | Ballroom | Enables Food + Poison → Bait crafting |
| **Trap Workshop** | Ballroom | Enables disabling traps for Trap Parts |
| **Lookout Post** | Grand Attic | Early Kuzco warning, Kuzco distraction |
| **Cat Barrier** | Grand Attic | Permanent Kuzco immunity |

Rats assigned to zones produce that zone's resource. Unassigned rats forage automatically.

## Research & Upgrades

Spent with Scrap currency. Knowledge carries over between attics based on Library zone level. 5 categories, ~3-5 upgrades each.

### Foraging
| Upgrade | Effect |
|---------|--------|
| Auto-scavenge speed | Mate/babies grab junk more often |
| Carrying capacity | Items per scavenge trip (1 → 3-4) |
| Detection range | Personal detection radius while wandering |

### Nesting
| Upgrade | Effect |
|---------|--------|
| Build speed | Zone construction/upgrade time reduced |
| Zone max level | Zones can be upgraded higher |
| Nest capacity | More rats per attic (stacks with base cap) |

### Crafting
| Upgrade | Effect |
|---------|--------|
| Bait efficiency | Less Food wasted per Bait crafted |
| Trap yield | More Trap Parts per disabled trap |
| Poison yield | More Poison per collected puddle |

### Threat Management
| Upgrade | Effect |
|---------|--------|
| Trap detection | Rats auto-avoid more traps |
| Trap disable speed | Faster to click-disarm a trap |
| Kuzco detection | Earlier warning before Kuzco arrives (works with Lookout Post) |
| Kuzco distraction | Thrown Food buys more safe time |
| Poison resistance | Faster poison recovery |

### Colony
| Upgrade | Effect |
|---------|--------|
| Rat speed | All rats move faster |
| Litter size | More babies per Litter |
| Recovery speed | Faster recovery from trap/poison at nest |
| Growth rate | Baby rats become workers faster |

## Terms

### Environment

- **Nest** — the player's base. Grows and upgrades over time through zones. Central visual element.
- **Attic** — the game environment. Contains scattered junk items to scavenge. New attics unlock as you progress.

### Resources

| Resource | Source | Early Use | Late Use |
|----------|--------|-----------|----------|
| **Fluff** | Cloth scraps | Nest zones, basic upgrades | High-tier upgrades, Nursery |
| **Scrap** | Paper bits | Research unlocks | Advanced research, Trap Workshop |
| **Food** | Food crumbs | Rat speed boost | Litter; combined with Poison → Bait for cat |
| **Shiny** | Shiny baubles | — | Mating Display, Ratopia funding |
| **Trap Parts** | Disabled traps | — | Cat Barrier upgrades |
| **Poison** | Collected safely | — | Lace Food → Bait → Kuzco gets sick |

### Threats

**Kuzco** (the Cat) — active threat. Patrols attic when present.
- **Trigger:** After Kuzco leaves, a random 2-3min cooldown. Once it expires, an activity-based check rolls every ~30s (more rats foraging = higher chance), with a recency floor so Kuzco never exceeds ~5min between end of cooldown and next visit.
- **If warned (Lookout Post active):** Kuzco detected before arrival. Rats hide, production pauses. During his visit, paws poke under the floorboards — click them to push back. Each click earns a few bonus Scrap (he knocks stuff loose). Keeps the player entertained during downtime. "All clear" sounds when Kuzco leaves.
- **If not warned (Lookout rats away rescuing):** Kuzco arrives unannounced. He chases and bats at any rats still out. Doesn't leave while rats are visible unless player throws Food to distract him.
- **While present (warned):** stays ~20s then leaves.
- **Player can lure with Food** — leaving plain Food in the open attracts Kuzco sooner (player controls timing).
- **Player can distract with Food** — click-throw Food at Kuzco, he stops to eat and leaves sooner (critical during unwarned visits).
- **Player can poison with Bait** — leaving Bait (Food+Poison) lures Kuzco, he gets sick, extended absence.
- **Countered permanently:** Cat Barrier (Grand Attic tier).

**Kuzco + trapped rat interaction:** If Kuzco is present when a rat is trapped, he bats at them. Rat escapes but recovery time doubles. No permadeath.

**Traps** — passive hazard, random spots on attic floor. Respawn after triggered or over time.
- **Storage Room:** High density. Rat hits trap → pinned, must be rescued by another rat or click. Rats assigned to Lookout Post auto-rescue trapped rats, but with a short delay — giving the player time to click and rescue faster manually. However, if Kuzco arrives while Lookout rats are out rescuing, you get no warning (they're away from their post). Rescued rat returns to nest, recovers ~30s. Teaches rescue mechanic.
- **Ballroom:** Medium density. Disable upgrade available → collect Trap Parts.
- **Grand Attic:** Very low density. Efficient disabling.
- **Eaves / Ratopia:** None.

**Poison** — passive hazard. Random puddles that despawn after a while (urgency to collect).
- **Storage Room:** Rat steps in puddle → slowed, staggers back. If carrying Food, Food is lost (spoiled). Contaminates Food storage on return → lose some stored Food. No Infirmary yet.
- **Ballroom:** Infirmary zone unlocked. Poisoned rat recovers there safely — no contamination, faster recovery. Safe poison collection upgrade available.
- **Grand Attic:** Efficient collection for Bait crafting.

### Automation Progression

1. **Manual** — single rat (you), always manual
2. **Mate** (Mating Display) — second rat arrives, auto-scavenges slowly
3. **Litter** (Food + Fluff invested, Nursery required) — baby rats hatch, auto-scavenge faster
4. **Full colony** — swarm of rats; player assigns priorities to zones, unassigned rats forage

### Progression Arc

- **Threat → Resource** — traps and poison transition from hazards to crafting materials as the player unlocks upgrades.
- **Mating Display** — first major goal. Triggers mate arrival and attic move.
- **Ratopia** — ultimate endgame state. Final nest completion triggers the game's ending.
